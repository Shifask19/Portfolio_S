## Problem

Small-scale farmers in India lack affordable, localised guidance on which crops to grow and how to diagnose plant diseases early, leading to yield losses and wasted inputs.

## My Role

I led a team of four, owned the AI pipeline and backend services, and coordinated front-end integration with Firebase.

## Approach & Architecture

- **Crop recommendation model** — trained a scikit-learn classifier on soil type, season and regional data to suggest the best-fit crop.
- **Disease detection** — a Python computer-vision model analyses leaf images uploaded through the React UI.
- **IoT layer** — hardware sensors feed real-time soil-moisture and temperature data into Firestore, which the dashboard reads live.
- **E-commerce module** — Firebase-backed product listings allow verified agri-input suppliers to list seeds and fertilisers directly to farmers.

## Challenges

- Labelled training data for regional crop varieties was scarce; we curated a small labelled set and used data augmentation.
- Keeping the Firebase bill zero required careful Firestore read/write rules and client-side caching.
