## Approach & Architecture

- **Detection** — YOLOv8 detects people in each video frame.
- **Tracking** — ByteTrack assigns persistent IDs across frames so each shopper is counted once even as they move between zones.
- **Backend** — FastAPI exposes endpoints that accept video segments and return analytics JSON (zone counts, dwell times, heatmap data).

## Challenges

- Real-time processing on hackathon-grade hardware required frame-skipping and resolution trade-offs.
- ByteTrack ID stitching across occlusion events needed careful threshold tuning.
